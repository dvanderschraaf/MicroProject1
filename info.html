Project Information

Overview

This interaction models exhaustion through movement. The user controls a square by moving the mouse, and movement drains an energy variable over time. As energy decreases, the square becomes smaller and slower to respond. Once the square runs out of energy, it stops moving, while slowly regenerating energy. This is the period to get as many points as you can quickly, without worrying about getting caught. Once the square has generated enough energy, the square will quickly attack.

You will notice that the background changes from Red to Green. The more red the background is, the more energy the square has. The more green the background is, the less energy the square has.
