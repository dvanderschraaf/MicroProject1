Project Information

Overview

This interaction models exhaustion through movement. The user controls a square by moving the mouse, and movement drains an energy variable over time. As energy decreases, the square becomes smaller and slower to respond. If the user stops moving, the energy slowly regenerates.

Instructions

DONT LET THE SQUARE TOUCH YOU - CLICK ON CIRCLES TO GET POINTS - GATHER POINTS TO GET A HIGHER SCORE
